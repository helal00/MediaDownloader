# Changelog

## 1.6.0.0
- My folders tab on the users' page: make, rename, move and delete folders inside your own folders.
- My files: move selected files to another of your folders (a film moves with its own folder and poster).
- Downloads can go straight into any of your folders; the page remembers the last one.

## 1.5.1.0
- A "Media Downloader" link in Jellyfin's side menu for everyone (Settings › People who may download; on by default). It is added as Jellyfin sends its web settings, so no Jellyfin file changes and it stays after Jellyfin updates.

## 1.5.0.0
- Users who do not manage the server can download too, at `<server>/MediaDownloader` (sign in with their Jellyfin account), each limited to folders the administrator gives them; they see only their own downloads and files, and may delete there if allowed.
- Downloads show who asked for them.

## 1.4.1.0
- Deleting also removes parent folders left empty.

## 1.4.0.0
- Tabbed page (Download, Downloads, Storage, Settings).
- Storage tab: free space, folder sizes, every file biggest first with who watched it, filters, delete through Jellyfin.

## 1.3.x
- Downloads from every site yt-dlp supports; link check with example links.

## 1.2.0
- "Open in Jellyfin to play".
